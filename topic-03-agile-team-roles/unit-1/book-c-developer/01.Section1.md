# What is a Developer?

In the Scrum framework, **Developers** are the team members responsible for building and delivering the product. This role goes beyond coding or technical work and includes all activities required to turn ideas into valuable increments of the product.

## Responsibilities of Developers

Developers contribute directly to the creation of the product. Their responsibilities include:

### 1. **Delivering a Done Increment**
   - **Adhering to Quality Standards:** Ensure all work meets the Definition of Done (DoD).
   - **Increment Creation:** Build usable increments of the product each sprint that meet stakeholder expectations.
   - **Testing:** Perform unit tests, integration tests, and other checks to ensure functionality and performance.

### 2. **Collaborating with the Team**
   - **Sprint Planning:** Estimate effort, break down tasks, and commit to sprint goals.
   - **Daily Stand-ups:** Share progress and discuss challenges with the team.
   - **Knowledge Sharing:** Collaborate and share expertise within the team.

### 3. **Refining the Backlog**
   - **Requirement Clarification:** Work with the Product Owner to refine and understand backlog items.
   - **Estimation:** Help estimate the complexity and effort required for backlog items.

### 4. **Participating in Scrum Events**
   - **Retrospectives:** Contribute to discussions about team improvements.
   - **Sprint Review:** Demonstrate completed work to stakeholders and gather feedback.

### 5. **Continuous Improvement**
   - **Skill Development:** Keep up-to-date with new technologies and best practices.
   - **Process Improvement:** Suggest and adopt process changes that enhance team efficiency.

## Skills and Attributes of a Great Developer

Developers need a mix of technical and interpersonal skills to succeed, such as:

- **Technical Expertise:** Proficiency in programming, testing, and debugging.
- **Problem-Solving:** Ability to analyze and resolve complex challenges.
- **Teamwork:** Willingness to collaborate and support team goals.
- **Adaptability:** Open to changing requirements and learning new tools.

## Challenges Faced by Developers

Developers face unique challenges, including:

- **Unclear Requirements:** Managing ambiguity in user stories or backlog items.
- **Time Constraints:** Delivering high-quality work within sprint deadlines.
- **Balancing Quality and Speed:** Ensuring robust solutions without compromising velocity.

---

By working together, the **Scrum Master**, **Product Owner**, and **Developers** form a cohesive team that leverages Agile principles to deliver high-value products effectively and efficiently.
