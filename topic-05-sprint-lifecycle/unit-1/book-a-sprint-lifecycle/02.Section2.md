# What is a Sprint Retrospective?

The **Sprint Retrospective** is a key Agile ceremony where the team reflects on the sprint, identifies areas for improvement, and plans actionable steps to enhance future performance. It fosters a culture of continuous improvement and open communication.

## Purpose of a Sprint Retrospective

- **Improve Processes:** Identify what worked well and what didn’t.
- **Enhance Collaboration:** Foster team cohesion by openly discussing challenges and successes.
- **Drive Continuous Improvement:** Create actionable plans to address issues and build on strengths.

## Format of a Sprint Retrospective

A typical retrospective focuses on answering three key questions:

1. **What went well during the sprint?**
2. **What didn’t go well?**
3. **What can we improve for the next sprint?**

The retrospective is usually held at the end of the sprint, after the sprint review, and is time-boxed to **1-2 hours** depending on the sprint length.

## Techniques for Retrospectives

- **Start, Stop, Continue:** Identify actions to start, stop, and continue in the next sprint.
- **Mad, Sad, Glad:** Discuss emotional responses to events in the sprint.
- **5 Whys:** Dig into the root causes of issues.
- **Dot Voting:** Prioritize topics the team feels are most critical.

## Benefits of a Sprint Retrospective

- Promotes open and honest communication.
- Builds trust and psychological safety within the team.
- Drives continuous improvement in team performance and processes.

## Tips for Effective Retrospectives

- Create a safe space for honest feedback.
- Focus on actionable takeaways.
- Rotate facilitation to encourage diverse perspectives.
- Follow up on previous retrospective actions to ensure accountability.
