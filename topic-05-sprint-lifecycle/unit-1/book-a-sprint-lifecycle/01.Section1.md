# What is a Daily Standup?

The **Daily Standup**, also known as the **Daily Scrum**, is a short, time-boxed meeting held by Agile teams to sync on progress, identify obstacles, and plan the day ahead. It is an essential ceremony in Scrum that promotes transparency, accountability, and team collaboration.

## Purpose of a Daily Standup

- **Team Alignment:** Ensure all team members are on the same page regarding sprint progress.
- **Impediment Identification:** Quickly surface and address any blockers.
- **Daily Planning:** Adjust plans based on current progress and priorities.

## Format of a Daily Standup

The standup is typically structured around three key questions for each team member:

1. **What did you do yesterday?**
2. **What will you do today?**
3. **Are there any blockers or impediments?**

The meeting is usually time-boxed to **15 minutes** and held at the same time and place daily.

## Benefits of a Daily Standup

- Promotes team transparency and collaboration.
- Keeps the team focused on sprint goals.
- Provides early visibility into potential risks or delays.

## Tips for Effective Standups

- Keep it brief and focused.
- Encourage active listening and avoid problem-solving during the meeting.
- Use a visible board or tool to track progress (e.g., a Kanban board).
- Ensure the standup is a team activity, not a status update for management.
